What is digital marketing?

Marketing That Is Planned, Measured And Explained

Digital marketing is the use of online channels such as search engines, paid ads, social media, email and content to attract customers and measure the results. For US businesses, Hesten Solutions combines local and national SEO, Google Ads, social media and content, with every channel tracked to calls, form fills and sales.

We build one joined-up plan across search, paid ads and social, so each channel supports the others. Every campaign has a clear goal, a budget you approve and reporting you can understand without a marketing degree.
