Frequently Asked Questions

Web Development Answers In Plain English

Clear answers on US pricing, timelines, WordPress vs custom builds, payments, accessibility and code ownership.

How much does web development cost in the USA?
Cost depends on the number of pages, custom features and integrations. We give a fixed written quote after a free consultation, so you know the price before work starts.
How long does it take to build a website?
A typical business website takes a few weeks. Online stores and web applications take longer. We confirm the timeline in writing before we begin.
Should I use WordPress or a custom build?
WordPress suits many business sites because it is flexible and easy to update. A custom build suits sites with complex features or unusual workflows. We recommend the option that fits your goals and budget.
Can you build an online store that takes US payments?
Yes. We build stores that work with payment providers such as Stripe, PayPal and Square, with US tax and shipping options set up to suit your business.
Will my website be accessible?
We build with WCAG accessibility guidelines in mind, including clear structure, keyboard use and readable contrast. We can review an existing site and list what needs fixing.
Who owns my website and code?
You do. Once the final invoice is paid, you own your website, content and custom code, and we hand over all logins.
Do you offer maintenance after launch?
Yes. We offer optional maintenance, security updates, backups and ongoing improvements.
